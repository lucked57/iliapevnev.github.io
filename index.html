Hello! 👋

I'm Ilia, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology graduate from STI College Baguio, Philippines. I'm originally from Moscow, Russia, where I first studied Information Systems and IT before moving to Baguio City to finish my degree.

My path into web development started back in 2018, when I built my first real client website with native PHP and kept supporting it for four years. Since then I've grown into a full-stack developer working mostly with Laravel, Vue.js and MySQL, from database design and back-end logic to smooth, responsive interfaces.

Besides coding, I have about two years of professional experience as a front-end developer, project administrator, service coordinator and project coordinator at companies like Xerox and Servionica. That taught me how real teams work: clear communication, deadlines, reporting and following SLAs.

Right now I'm looking for a Junior Software / Web Developer position where I can bring both my technical skills and my work ethic to a growing team.
